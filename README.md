# A'One of One: A'ja Wilson Contract History

**Built by Kris Smith · The Black Techie**

A data visualization tracking A'ja Wilson's WNBA salary history across eight seasons against her on-court accolades and the historic 2026 supermax contract that finally paid her like the greatest player in the game.

---

## About the Piece

This piece was built on April 15, 2026 the day A'ja Wilson signed the largest contract in WNBA history under the new CBA. The viz frames the financial story the way it should be told: awards first, salary second. The gap between those two columns is the story.

---

## Context

- The new CBA raised the WNBA salary cap from $1.5M to $7M
- The supermax rose from $249,244 in 2025 to $1.4M base in 2026
- In 2025, A'ja was not in the top 25 highest-paid WNBA players teammate Jewell Loyd earned more
- Her 8-season career total ($1,031,642) is less than her new single-year salary ($1,670,000)
- She is the first player in WNBA history to win MVP, Finals MVP, DPOY, and a championship in the same season (2025)
- This is the 30th Anniversary Season of the WNBA
